KARTIPELAGO: THE SHATTERED PACT
The Adventure mode script: every cutscene, in the order it plays.

Made from the game's own scene data (src/adventure/scenes.ts and
staged.ts): change a line there and this script changes with it. Lines
are as they appear on screen. Captions are read by the narrator. Stage
directions, in brackets, come from the 3D staging of each scene.

CAST
  YOU             Your racer (Tiko, Brakka, Pebble or Zephyr; a champion's cousin plays a champion's part).
  ELDER MOSSBACK  Keeper of the Pact: he sends the champion. Wears the Founders' Horn at his hip, and its secret.
  LUMEN           Mossback's ward, raised from an egg found in the river. Always hooded, always asking. Really a dragon, his wings hidden under the robe.
  REX             Dinosaur Valley's champion. A cocky lone racer, no crew. Your first.
  BRACHIO         The Snow Kingdom's champion, Rimefang's captive.
  STEGO           Desert World's champion, Scorchwind's star act.
  RAPTOR          Tropic Isles' champion, Tidewyrm's 'treasure'.
  CINDERJAW       Dragon of the valley: the brute. All fire, no patience.
  RIMEFANG        Dragon of the snow: the lawyer. Cold, precise, never breaks a rule.
  SCORCHWIND      Dragon of the desert: the showman. Vain, theatrical.
  TIDEWYRM        Dragon of the isles: the trickster. Greedy, sly, charming.
  KING PYRRHAX    The Dragon King. He lost the First Race young, stunned by the horn, and has beaten every champion since.
  SKETCHY GUARD   The smugglers' guards at the underground games. They sell tunnels.
  GUARD           The dragons' own guards: Dragon Kingdom folk in hooded tunics.
  THE CROWD       The Tropic Isles' captives and townsfolk.

SCENE 1. THE FIRST RACE   [first_race]
------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHEN: A new file is started, before you pick your racer.
WHERE: A storybook page, inked pictures turning with each line.

    [Dragons sweep across the page.]
    NARRATOR (CAPTION)
        A THOUSAND YEARS AGO, THE DRAGONS AND THE FIRST RACING CREWS
        WENT TO WAR OVER THE FOUR LANDS.

    NARRATOR (CAPTION)
        AT LAST THEY AGREED: ONE RACE WOULD END THE WAR. BEFORE IT
        BEGAN, BOTH SIDES SWORE TO ITS RULES. THEY CALLED THAT OATH THE
        DRAGON PACT.

    NARRATOR (CAPTION)
        RULE ONE: WHOEVER LOST THE RACE WOULD LEAVE THE FOUR LANDS AND
        LIVE BEHIND A BORDER. AND NEITHER SIDE WOULD EVER HARM THE OTHER
        AGAIN.

    NARRATOR (CAPTION)
        RULE TWO: EVERY HUNDRED YEARS, THE WINNERS MUST SEND THEIR BEST
        RACER, A CHAMPION, TO RACE THE LOSERS' KING.

    NARRATOR (CAPTION)
        RULE THREE: IF A HUNDRED YEARS PASS AND NO CHAMPION COMES, THE
        WINNERS LOSE THE LANDS, UNTIL A CHAMPION BEATS THE KING AND WINS
        THEM BACK.

    [A horn blasts.]
    NARRATOR (CAPTION)
        THE DRAGONS FLEW FASTER THAN ANYTHING ALIVE. BUT AT THE LAST
        TURN, A HORN SOUNDED...

    NARRATOR (CAPTION)
        ...THE DRAGONS FALTERED, AND THE CREWS CROSSED THE LINE FIRST.

    [Golden light.]
    NARRATOR (CAPTION)
        THE DRAGONS KEPT THEIR OATH. THEY WITHDREW BEHIND THE BORDER, TO
        THE DRAGON KINGDOM.

    NARRATOR (CAPTION)
        A PACT STONE STANDS IN EACH LAND, AND THE HEART STONE IN THE
        DRAGON KINGDOM. WHILE THEY STAND, THE PACT HOLDS.
