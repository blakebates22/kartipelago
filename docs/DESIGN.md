# Design document

This is a living document. Update it when a decision changes.

## Vision

A bright, cheerful adventure kart racer that runs in any modern browser. Players explore
an island hub, pick a vehicle, and race rivals across land, water and sky.

## Pillars

1. **Feel first.** Driving must be fun on a flat empty plane before anything else is built.
2. **Three ways to race.** Car, hovercraft and plane are equally viable, and many tracks
   support more than one.
3. **Adventure, not just a menu.** Races are reached by driving around a hub world.
4. **Instant to play.** No install, no login, loads fast, works with keyboard or gamepad.

## Core mechanics (planned)

- Drifting with a mini-boost on release.
- Boost pads and a start-line boost for good timing.
- Item pickups, color-coded by type, that power up if you collect the same color again.
- Character roster with distinct stats (acceleration, top speed, handling).
- Boss races against a big rival character at the end of each world.
- Optional collectible challenges on tracks you've already beaten.

## Visual style

Low-poly, flat- or lightly-shaded, saturated colors, chunky readable shapes. This keeps
assets achievable for a small team and runs well on modest hardware.

## Out of scope for now

Online multiplayer, mobile touch controls, cutscenes, voice acting. These may come later,
but only as a deliberate decision recorded here.

## Open questions

- Characters: what kind of creatures, and how many in the first roster?
- World theme for the first island.
- Music direction.
