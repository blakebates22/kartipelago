# The first Tracks menus (archived)

`worldSelect.ts` (Choose a World: wooden boards with track mosaics) and `trackSelect.ts`
(the track page: a big picture on parchment, a row of tiles, the race type and Start),
kept for reference. They are not built or loaded by the game.

They were replaced in October 2026 by the Ledger (`src/screens/tracks.ts`): one screen
with world bookmarks, the list (Trophy Race, then the tracks) and the chosen one's
picture and details, on an ancient-map background.

To see them again, check out a commit from before the change (e.g. bb494a2), or copy a
file back into `src/screens/` (imports are relative to it).
